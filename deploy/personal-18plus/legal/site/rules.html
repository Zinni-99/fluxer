Community rules

This is a private, adults-only (18+) server. Joining means you agree to these rules. Breaking them can get you removed without warning.

1. Adults only

  • You must be 18 or older, and at least the age of majority where you live if that is higher.
  • Don't lie about your age. If we have reason to think someone is under 18, we remove them right away.
  • Never share anything that shows, or appears to show, a minor in a sexual way. This includes drawings, AI-generated images and "age play" content that depicts minors. We delete it, ban the account and report it to the authorities (in the US, the NCMEC CyberTipline; in the UK, the IWF).

2. Consent comes first

  • Only share explicit images or video of yourself, or of people who agreed to it being shared here. No hidden-camera, leaked, "revenge" or stolen content.
  • No AI or edited explicit images of real people who didn't agree to them.
  • If someone in your content asks for it to be removed, remove it.
  • "No" means no, in DMs too. Don't send explicit content to people who haven't asked for it.

3. Keep explicit content in its place

  • Explicit content goes only in channels marked NSFW (age-restricted).
  • Profile pictures, names and status messages stay safe for work.

4. Respect people

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying.
  • No doxxing: never share someone's real name, address, workplace or photos that identify them without permission.
  • What's shared here stays here. Don't screenshot or repost other members' content outside the server.

5. Nothing illegal

  • No content or activity that is illegal where the server is hosted or where you live, including sex trafficking, sale of sexual services where illegal, non-consensual content, bestiality or extreme violence.
  • No spam, scams, malware or selling.

Reporting

Use the report button on a message or profile. Reports go straight to the admins. Anything involving a minor is treated as the top priority.

The admins have the final say on these rules and may update them. We'll post when they change.
